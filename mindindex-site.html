Frequently asked

Is it free to search the directory?+

Yes. Searching, filtering and contacting professionals is free for anyone using the directory.

How are professionals verified?+

We check each professional's registration number against the HPCSA or SACSSP register before listing, and re-verify annually.

What's the difference between a psychologist, counsellor, psychiatrist and social worker?+

Psychologists and registered counsellors provide talk therapy and assessment. Psychiatrists are medical doctors who can diagnose and prescribe medication. Social workers support individuals, families and communities. Each profile states the practitioner's registration category, and the match quiz can help you decide where to start.

Do professionals pay to rank higher in results?+

No. Results are ordered by relevance to your filters, never by payment.

Do you accept medical aid?+

We don't process payments or claims. Each profile shows whether a professional works with medical aid, charges private rates or offers a sliding scale, so you can check before you book.

Can I see someone online?+

Yes. Each profile states whether the professional works online, in person, or both.

What if I'm not sure what kind of help I need?+

That's what the match quiz is for. Pick the closest focus area, and a practitioner can help refine things in a first session. Many offer a short intro call.

Is this a crisis service?+

No. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call the SADAG Suicide Crisis Line on 0800 567 567 or go to your nearest emergency room.

Does MindIndex recommend one professional over another?+

No. We don't rank by who pays us and we don't make clinical recommendations. The directory gives you the information to decide for yourself.

What happens if a registration lapses?+

Listings are re-verified annually, and a profile is removed if a professional's registration lapses or is withdrawn.

About MindIndex Directory

An independent record of who to trust with your mind.

Reaching out for help is hard enough. MindIndex Directory gives South Africans one place to find registered mental health professionals and compare them on their own merits, not on who shouldn't loudest.

Why we started

Looking for a therapist or psychiatrist is hard, and it usually happens when you're already struggling. Most of us end up asking a friend, making call after call, or choosing whoever's website looks the most reassuring. That's a lot to carry when you're already worn out.

We started MindIndex because we believe finding help shouldn't be this difficult. Here you can look for a professional by where they practise, what they specialise in, which languages they speak and what they charge.

You've already taken the hardest step by looking for support. We want the next one to be a little easier.

What we check

Everyone deserves meaningful information before choosing who to open up to.

Every professional listed on MindIndex is subject to our stated verification requirements, including checking their HPCSA or SACSSP registration number before listing and re-checking it annually. We do not accept commission for referrals, and professionals cannot pay for a higher position in search results.

MindIndex is a directory, not a clinical service. We don't diagnose, treat or make clinical recommendations, and we are not an emergency service.

Frequently asked

How much does it cost to be listed?+

Pricing is shown above, charged annually. There are no setup fees and no commission on your fees.

Who can be listed?+

Psychologists, registered counsellors, psychometrists, psychiatrists and social workers in good standing with the HPCSA or SACSSP.

What do you check before listing me?+

We verify your registration number against the HPCSA or SACSSP register before your profile goes live, and re-check annually to keep listings current.

How long does the application take to be approved?+

Most applications are reviewed within a few working days of submission, once your registration checks out.

Can I update my profile after I'm listed?+

Yes. Get in touch and we'll update your details, focus areas, fees or photo at any time.

How do clients find and contact me?+

Clients filter by focus area, location and language, or take the match quiz, and reach you directly through the details on your profile. There's no call centre or middleman in between.

Can I list more than one focus area?+

Yes, your profile can show every focus area, population and approach that applies to your practice.

Can I cancel my listing?+

Yes, there's no long-term contract. Get in touch and we'll remove your profile.
